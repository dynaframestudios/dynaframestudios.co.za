Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, process, hosting and payment plans.

Pricing & Packages
How much does a website cost?

Every project starts from a base 1‑page website at R2,000. From there you add exactly what you need — extra pages, logo design, custom images, a booking tool, an admin panel, or full e‑commerce. Use the instant quote calculator on our Web Design page to see live pricing as you build your package.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Every line item is shown upfront in the quote calculator and on your invoice before you pay anything. The only ongoing cost is hosting/maintenance for sites with an admin panel or e‑commerce (R300/month), which is disclosed clearly whenever it applies.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. Alongside the standard 50% deposit / 50% on completion option, we offer month‑to‑month payment tiers so you can spread the setup cost over 3 months or 6 months interest‑free, in addition to any ongoing hosting fee. Select your preferred plan in the quote calculator on the Web Design page and it will be reflected on your invoice.

Our Process
What does the build process actually look like?

Every project follows the same four stages: Design (we scope your requirements and plan the layout), Demo (you review a working preview and request changes, free of charge), Hosting (we set up your domain, hosting and any admin/e‑commerce backend), and Final Product (your finished, live website is handed over). See the visual breakdown on our Web Design page.

How long does a project take?

A basic site typically takes 2–3 days depending on the number of pages. Sites with an admin panel take 6–8 days, and full e‑commerce builds take 10–13 days depending on how many products you're listing. Need it faster? Ask about our 36‑hour rush turnaround for basic sites.

Do I get to review the site before it goes live?

Yes — every project includes a free demo/consultation stage. You'll see a working preview and can request adjustments before we move to hosting and final delivery.

Hosting & Ownership
Who owns the website once it's finished?

Once your invoice is paid in full, the finished website and its design become your property. We only retain the right to showcase completed work in our portfolio. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance?

Yes. Basic informational sites include free ongoing maintenance. Sites with an admin panel or e‑commerce functionality include server hosting and maintenance for R300/month, billed alongside your chosen payment plan.

Will my site show up on Google?

Every website we build includes free Google Business Profile setup, optimised meta tags and descriptions, mobile‑responsive design and a submitted sitemap — the core fundamentals of local SEO — at no extra charge.

PC Builds & Repairs
Can I get a custom gaming PC built too?

Yes — visit our PC Builds page to configure a custom Intel or AMD gaming, workstation or budget rig with transparent, component‑by‑component pricing.

Do you repair phones and computers too?

Yes — we offer phone repairs in Midrand and computer repairs in Braamfontein. Contact us with your device issue for a quick assessment.

We're happy to walk you through pricing, timelines or your project scope directly.
